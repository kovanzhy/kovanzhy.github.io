About

I'm a senior fullstack engineer with 7+ years of experience delivering enterprise software. I currently work as a B2B contractor for clients across different industries.

My primary stack is .NET / .NET Core on the back end, React on the front end. I build to clean architecture standards — Domain-Driven Design, SOLID, test-driven — and care about code that stays maintainable over time, not just on the day it ships.

Earlier in my career I built cross-platform systems in C++ with CUDA and OpenCV — GPU computing, image processing, Linux. That low-level foundation informs how I think about performance and system design today.

I work with modern AI across the board — cloud and local models, open-source and commercial — both in my own workflows and integrated into team environments. Automating the right things, at the right level, is part of how I think about engineering. I speak Romanian, English, Ukrainian, and Russian.
